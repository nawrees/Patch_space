Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your first and last name, email address, phone number, address, and password. We also store data generated by your use of the platform: course progress, lesson completion, lab activity and ratings, and streaks and badges.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account (authentication is handled by Supabase).
  • To deliver courses and track your progress through them.
  • To operate the hands-on labs tied to your account.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the platform.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it to third parties.

3. Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Access to it is restricted by row-level security policies scoped to your account and role.

4. Cookies and local storage

Patch Space doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember interface preferences like theme and sound settings.

5. Your rights

You can review and update most of your account information from your profile page. To request a copy of your data or to have your account deleted, contact us.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continuing to use Patch Space after a change means you accept the update.
